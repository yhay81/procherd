# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, kindness, respect for differing opinions,
constructive feedback, responsibility after mistakes, and focus on community
health.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling,
insults, personal attacks, public or private harassment, publishing private
information without permission, or conduct reasonably considered
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders clarify and enforce these standards and may remove, edit, or
reject contributions not aligned with this Code. They will communicate
moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the
project in public. Report unacceptable behavior confidentially through
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/yhay81/procherd/security/advisories/new)
with `Conduct` at the start of the title.

Consequences follow the Contributor Covenant community impact ladder:
correction, warning, temporary ban, and permanent ban. Severity, repetition,
and community impact determine the response.
